**What’s the difference between AI-generated jazz and human-composed and performed jazz?**

JazzBloom begins with a small experiment: a piece of music generated with Lyria, prompted by an interest in Miles Davis’s *So What*. That experiment opened a question we do not yet know how to answer.

**Where are the similarities, and where are the differences?** What would we need to learn about jazz to describe them with more care?

## A starting example

The project author supplied the Lyria-generated *So What — Minimal Modal Trio*.

![[audio/lyria/v1/so-what-minimal-modal-trio.mp3]]

[[Motivation/So What|So What · Miles Davis]] is the reference recording for this example. Listen to the reference and the generated piece. Keep what you heard separate from what you think it might mean.

This example is a starting point for the broader question. The comparison remains open.
