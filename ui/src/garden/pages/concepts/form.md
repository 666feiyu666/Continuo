Form concerns how music is organized over time. A familiar phrase returning, a change in texture, or a new musical voice can become a landmark as we listen.

Alongside the third chapter of *How to Listen to Jazz*, choose one landmark and wait for it to return. Does it come back unchanged? Do you recognize it through a different sound or a different phrase?

## A question for our comparison

How do the two pieces unfold? A similar surface sound might coexist with a different sequence of events. Before explaining either piece, we can begin by marking what we hear and when we hear it.
