Timbre gives us a name for the character of a sound: what lets us hear a difference between instruments or between ways of playing them, even at a similar pitch.

Alongside the second chapter of *How to Listen to Jazz*, try following one voice in a recording. You do not need the right descriptive word immediately. Start with what you notice: a sharp attack, a breathy edge, a ringing tail, or a change as a phrase develops.

## A question for our comparison

When two sounds seem similar, what are you responding to? The instrument you recognize, the beginning of each note, the tone that follows, or the way the phrase is played?

Listen to [[recordings/so-what|the starting recording]], then return to [[starting-question|the generated piece and our open question]]. Attach a listening moment to an observation if it helps you locate what you mean.
