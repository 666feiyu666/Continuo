JazzBloom is a small digital humanities project about learning to listen to jazz. It starts with [[starting-question|a question raised by generated music]], and grows through reading, listening, and making connections.

## Pages and traces

Each page can hold a question, a concept, a recording, or a reading note. Links connect the pages, and “Linked from” shows where a page is referred to elsewhere in the garden. The chapter navigation offers one path through them.

An observation is a personal trace attached to a page. It can include selected text, a moment from your recording, and a connection to another page. Observations are saved in this browser and can be exported. The author’s Lyria study is included here. You can also select another audio file to play locally; after a refresh you will need to select that file again.

## Reading alongside Gioia

Ted Gioia’s *How to Listen to Jazz* provides a guide for our learning. These pages are original companion writing, not quotations, translations, or a replacement for the book. The chapter labels are our own navigation labels.

The opening question remains unanswered. Listening notes are observations and interpretations, not automatic measurements of musical similarity. The project author supplied the Lyria-generated *So What — Minimal Modal Trio*. Its exact generation prompt has not been supplied. This prototype presents the recording for listening, without a completed musical comparison.
